# India-CPI-Inflation-Analysis | Excel Data Analytics Project
An Excel-based analysis of India's Consumer Price Index (CPI), covering inflation trends, food prices, COVID-19 impact, and imported oil price correlations.

## 📌 Project Overview

An Excel-based data analytics project analyzing India's Consumer Price Index (CPI), inflation trends, category-level movements, and the relationship between major economic events and inflation.

The objective is to identify inflation patterns, analyze price changes, and translate findings into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

1. **CPI Basket Contribution:** Analyze broader CPI categories and identify their relative contributions using the latest available data.
2. **Year-over-Year Inflation Trends:** Analyze YoY CPI movements from 2017 onward and identify the year with the highest inflation rate.
3. **Food Inflation Analysis:** Investigate monthly food inflation trends for the 12 months ending May 2023.
4. **COVID-19 Impact Analysis:** Compare inflation trends before and after March 2020, focusing on food, healthcare, and essential services.
5. **Oil Price Correlation Analysis:** Explore the relationship between imported oil price fluctuations and category-level CPI movements during 2021–2023.

1. Reviewed the CPI dataset and business requirements.

3. Handled missing values where appropriate.

5. Calculated inflation growth rates using Excel formulas.

8. Organized findings according to the five business objectives.
